Inter and Montserrat are the included fallback fonts. They are distributed under
the SIL Open Font License; see their upstream projects for copyright notices.

Paid Proxima Nova and TikTok fonts are NOT included. Only add these if you hold
the appropriate license. Supported local filenames are listed in app/renderer.py.
They are excluded by .gitignore. Restart your own local server after adding fonts.

On non-Mac systems, UI cards use the included Inter font when Apple's system fonts
or Georgia are unavailable. That fallback can look different from the Mac version.
